# 02 · Design direction

## Why now
Every feature gets its own design session. Without a shared visual language, each session would argue the basics all over again.

## What we know
- Dash gets a **new visual language**. It doesn't reuse any existing GGC design system.
- Kitze's Bench is the inspiration: warm and airy, with rounded cards, cover images, avatars and soft shadows.
- You've said before that dark mode should be dark **grey**, not black.

## Questions
1. **Density:** calm "life at a glance" (like Bench) or a dense console? Does it differ between home and the deeper views?
2. **Personality:** avatars and agent personas, or ids and status dots?
3. **Type and colour:** a starting palette and typeface. Do we want a mood board session first?
4. **Component approach:** build our own Svelte components with design tokens from the start, or use a headless library (Bits UI or Melt UI) underneath?
5. **Mock tooling:** HTML pages under `design/` in the repo, Claude Design canvases, or Svelte straight away with fake data?
6. **Slice-1 surfaces:** which ones matter most — home, a GitHub inbox, a Today view?

## Done when
The visual direction and mock tooling are agreed, and there's a short list of slice-1 screens to design.
